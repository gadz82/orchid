The best way to get help with Orchid is to open an issue on GitHub, where the maintainer and community can respond in public. For direct contact, reach out on LinkedIn.

- **GitHub issues** — bug reports, feature requests, and questions: [Open an issue →](https://github.com/gadz82/orchid/issues)
- **LinkedIn** — direct contact with the maintainer: [Francesco Marchesini →](https://www.linkedin.com/in/francescomarchesini/)
